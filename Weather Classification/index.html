Students can follow the complete classification process.

📚
STEP 2

Training

AI learns patterns from previous weather examples.

🔎

Compare

New weather data is compared with learned patterns.

🎯

Classify

AI assigns the data to Sunny, Rainy or Cloudy.

How does the classification backend work?

The AI receives four features: Temperature, Humidity, Wind Speed and Atmospheric Pressure. It looks for patterns and assigns the new data to a category.
