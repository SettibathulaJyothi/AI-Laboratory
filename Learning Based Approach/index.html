Give the AI some unlabelled balls. The AI will try to find groups based on ball size.

Unlabelled Data

💡 What is happening?

The balls are unlabelled data. The AI does not know whether a ball is a basketball, volleyball, football, tennis ball, etc.

Each ball has different properties, but for this experiment the AI is given only one important feature: size.

The AI compares the sizes and finds balls that are similar to each other.

After training, the AI automatically creates groups called clusters.

This is an example of unsupervised learning.
